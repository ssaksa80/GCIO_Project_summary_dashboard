The options

Each is a complete identity — ground, accent, and mood — over the same accessibility-validated chart palette, so the numbers read identically in all four.

A Obsidian Midnight executive Current default

Deep charcoal with an electric-blue accent and a faint aurora glow. A command-center presence — strongest in low-lit offices and on wall displays, where it reads as calm control.

B Platinum Ivory boardroom

Warm ivory, navy ink, gold detail. The print-adjacent formality of a bound board pack — strongest in bright rooms and on projectors, and the closest match to printed briefing material.

C Sapphire Navy & gold

Deep sapphire ground with gold accents — ceremonial and sovereign. The most brand-forward of the four; it carries institutional weight in stakeholder and government settings.

D Emerald Graphite & jade

Graphite ground with a jade accent — modern, understated, quietly technical. The most contemporary read; it signals engineering confidence without raising its voice.

Identical in every option

The theme changes the dress, never the substance. Every option ships with:

Live 24×7 ingestion

Drop Excel files — single or bulk, .xlsx/.xls/.csv — into a watched folder or upload in-app; every open screen updates within a second.

Intelligent Excel reading

Column headers, RAG values, date formats, and money notation are matched intelligently across differently-authored workbooks.

Written executive briefing

A machine-written narrative — headline, developments, wins, watch-list, outlook — recomposed for daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly views.

Full project drill-down

Owner, sponsor, approval-to-forecast dates, budget burn, milestone timeline, risk register, update feed, and the parent→child project chain.

Boardroom exports

One click produces a styled Excel workbook, a Word briefing with cover page and per-project briefs, or a self-contained HTML brief — with chart images embedded.

Validated chart color

The series palette passes color-vision-deficiency and contrast gates on every theme's surface, so charts stay legible for every reader.

The drill-down, in two of the options

Selecting any project opens its complete record — the same depth in all four themes.

How to decide

Every option is live now — the dashboard's top bar switches between them instantly, so they can also be compared side-by-side in the product before deciding. The chosen option becomes the default identity for all users; individuals keep the ability to switch unless we lock it.
